This module provides the **Ukrainian National Classifier of Occupations
DK 003:2010** (Класифікатор професій ДК 003:2010), the official
Ukrainian standard maintained by Derzhspozhyvstandart of Ukraine.

The classifier ships with:

- the full DK 003:2010 catalog hierarchy with around 823 categories
  organised in 4 levels (sections, subsections, classes, subclasses);
- over 9,150 specific job titles with their official KP codes
  (Класифікатор професій);
- hierarchical search and grouping by catalog node.

For Ukrainian HR specialists, the presence of DK 003:2010 is a baseline
expected feature of any HR software used in Ukraine. Other modules can
reference l10n.ua.hr.job.classification records via Many2one fields to
attach official profession codes to their own records.

Usage
=====

After installation, the classifier is accessible under **Employees →
Configuration → Classifications**:

- **Job Classifications** — flat list of all profession entries, with
  search by name or KP code.
- **Classification Catalog** — hierarchical view of the DK 003:2010
  category tree, with profession counts per node.

The catalog is preloaded automatically at install time. No additional
configuration is required.

Other credits
-------------

The classifier data is based on the Ukrainian National Classifier of
Occupations DK 003:2010 (Класифікатор професій ДК 003:2010), an official
state standard maintained by Derzhspozhyvstandart of Ukraine.

**Legal basis:** DK 003:2010 was approved by Order No. 327 of the State
Committee of Ukraine for Technical Regulation and Consumer Policy dated
28 July 2010 (наказ Держспоживстандарту України №327 від 28.07.2010), in
force since 1 November 2010.

**Source:** Official current version at
https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/rada/show/va327609-10

**Copyright status:** As an official state standard, DK 003:2010 is
exempt from copyright protection under Ukraine's Law on Copyright and
Related Rights (Article 8), which excludes official documents of state
authorities and non-original factual databases. Redistribution of the
classifier data alongside this module under AGPL-3 is therefore lawful.
