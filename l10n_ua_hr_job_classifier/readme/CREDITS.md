The classifier data is based on the Ukrainian National Classifier of
Occupations DK 003:2010 (Класифікатор професій ДК 003:2010), an
official state standard maintained by Derzhspozhyvstandart of Ukraine.

**Legal basis:** DK 003:2010 was approved by Order No. 327 of the
State Committee of Ukraine for Technical Regulation and Consumer
Policy dated 28 July 2010 (наказ Держспоживстандарту України №327
від 28.07.2010), in force since 1 November 2010.

**Source:** Official current version at
https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/rada/show/va327609-10

**Copyright status:** As an official state standard, DK 003:2010 is
exempt from copyright protection under Ukraine's Law on Copyright and
Related Rights (Article 8), which excludes official documents of
state authorities and non-original factual databases. Redistribution
of the classifier data alongside this module under AGPL-3 is
therefore lawful.
