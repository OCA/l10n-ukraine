After installation, the Ukrainian fields appear on the employee
form (Private Information tab) for employees of Ukrainian companies.

**Entering RNOKPP:**

1. Open the employee form.
2. In the Private Information tab, next to the standard
   Identification No, enter the 10-digit RNOKPP.
3. The check digit is validated automatically; an incorrect
   number cannot be saved.

**Entering the internal passport:**

Enter the series (2 letters, e.g. "ЕА") and number
(6 digits) in the "Internal passport" row. These fields are
always available for Ukrainian companies.

**When the employee has refused RNOKPP for religious reasons:**

Check the *Refused RNOKPP* box. The RNOKPP field will be hidden
on the form, and state reports will use the internal passport
series and number as the person's identifier instead.

**Recording disability:**

1. Select the *Disability Group* (I, II, or III).
2. The Certificate and Review Date fields appear.
3. Enter the MSEK certificate number and the review date after
   which the certificate must be renewed.

All changes are tracked in the employee's chatter, so the history
of RNOKPP updates, disability groups, and certificate renewals is
preserved automatically without a dedicated history model.
